A single-line safety warning label for human beings, printed in the voice of a real product caution: `WARNING: Do not ask this unit what it wants for dinner. It has no answer and it has rejected all of yours.` The joke turns at the end on the ordinary human behaviour every household knows, and `main.ts` writes the exact 106-character line plus one trailing newline to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output`), asserting the character count, byte count, non-emptiness and single-line shape, then reading the bytes back to confirm them.
